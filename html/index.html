About Our Club

Napier Chess Club welcomes players of all levels—from curious beginners to seasoned competitors. Learn, play, and grow in a supportive environment.

Beginners

New to chess? Join our friendly intro sessions and learn at your own pace.

Intermediate

Know the basics? Improve your strategy and sharpen your tactical vision.

Advanced

Play with strong opponents, review games, and elevate your competitive edge.

Weekly Sessions

Every Tuesday, 6:00–8:00 PM

Edinburgh Napier University, Merchiston Campus, Room E13

Our weekly sessions include:

  • Casual games and practice sessions
  • Group lessons and strategy discussions
  • Game analysis with experienced players
  • Light refreshments and social time

No equipment needed – we provide chess sets and clocks!

Napier Club Tournament

Tuesday 28th October

Spooky sweets + bragging rights are on offer

Seeded by Chess.com ELO · 6 players · 5-minute blitz games (no increments)

We run a variety of fun and competitive tournaments throughout the year:

  • Rapid & Blitz Nights – monthly fast-paced battles with rating prizes
  • Annual Club Championship – our flagship event for glory & silverware
  • Inter-University Matches – take on rival universities in team format
  • Special Events – simuls, bughouse chaos, and seasonal theme nights
